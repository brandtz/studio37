## 3. Acknowledgment of ordinary site and handling risks

The customer understands that delivery, installation, and furniture assembly may involve carrying or positioning heavy or bulky items; lifting; hand or power tools; fasteners; stairs, thresholds, tight turns, elevators, and other access constraints; and working near floors, walls, fixtures, and personal property. These activities can present a risk of accidental injury or property damage, particularly where access is restricted or conditions are concealed or not disclosed.

Studio 37 may pause or decline work if the access route or work area appears unsafe, the item cannot be moved safely, or the actual conditions differ materially from those described before the visit. The parties should address any change in scope, site conditions, or additional charges in writing before proceeding where practicable.

## 4. Customer responsibilities

Before work begins, the customer agrees to:

- Confirm that they own the property or are authorized to arrange the work and permit access.
- Provide accurate information about the item, access route, stairs, elevators, parking, building rules, and known site restrictions.
- Identify known fragile or damaged floors, walls, trim, fixtures, utilities, concealed conditions, and other hazards that could affect the work.
- Clear the access route and work area, protect or move fragile and valuable belongings, and keep children, visitors, and pets away from the work area.
- Identify any required building approvals, permissions, or access arrangements that are the customer's responsibility.
- Be present, or appoint an adult representative, to review placement and apparent pre-existing conditions before work begins and to inspect the completed work.

If these preparations are incomplete or a hazard is discovered, Studio 37 may stop and reschedule or revise the work by written agreement.

## 5. Existing conditions and reporting concerns

Before work begins, the customer and Studio 37 should note any visible pre-existing damage or unusual conditions relevant to the work. The customer should promptly tell Studio 37 about any concern noticed during or immediately after the service so the parties can document and address it. This section does not decide responsibility for damage or limit any rights that cannot legally be limited.
