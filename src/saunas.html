Cedar · PNW-Built

Sauna season, dialed in.

Studio 37’s cedar sauna kits are built right here in Eastern Oregon, then delivered and set up at your site. We’ve simplified the process — one package covers the sauna itself, delivery, full setup, and electrical work where it’s needed (pulled and inspected under licensed sub). Most installs don’t need an inspection at all when sited on an existing pad or patio.

Five core configurations, all in genuine western red cedar with full heater, benching, glass door, and warm low-voltage lighting. Lead time is 6–8 weeks from deposit. Pricing scales with size and whether a concrete pad is required.

How it works

From quote to first fire.

  1. 1. Quote. Tell Drew the size you want and where you’re siting it. He’ll quote a flat package price including any pad and electrical work.
  2. 2. Deposit. 50% deposit confirms your spot in the build queue. Lead time begins.
  3. 3. Build. 6–8 weeks in the Studio 37 shop. We’ll send progress shots.
  4. 4. Site prep. If a pad is needed, it’s poured the week before delivery.
  5. 5. Delivery & setup. One-day install. Electrical inspection (if any) is scheduled the same week.
  6. 6. Walkthrough. Drew walks you through first-fire and care. You’re in.
