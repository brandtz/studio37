02 · Specialty

Slab Flattening

$85 / hour

Drew runs a CNC router sled setup capable of flattening live edge slabs, figured blanks, and large panels that a standard planer can't touch. Whether you've been sitting on a slab for years or you're sourcing something new, he can get it flat, square, and ready for your finish.

This is a specialty service most woodworking shops in the region can't offer. If you've been hunting for someone with a proper router sled setup, you found him.

  • Live edge slab flattening
  • Figured and highly figured blanks
  • Large panel flattening (beyond planer capacity)
  • River table blanks and epoxy-ready prep
  • Slab surfacing for countertops and dining tables

Who books this service: Homeowners with a slab they want made into a dining table or countertop; furniture makers and woodworkers who don't own a sled; interior designers sourcing statement pieces.

Typical turnaround: 1–3 business days depending on queue.
