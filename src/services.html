02 · Specialty

Slab Flattening

$85 / hour

Drew runs a router gantry setup capable of flattening live edge slabs, figured blanks, and large panels that a standard planer can't touch, up to 50" wide and 18' long. Whether you've been sitting on a slab for years or you're sourcing something new, he can get it flat, square, and ready for your finish.

This is a specialty service most woodworking shops in the region can't offer. If you've been hunting for someone with a proper router sled setup, you found him.

  • Live edge slab flattening
  • Figured and highly figured blanks
  • Large panel flattening (beyond planer capacity)
  • River table blanks and epoxy-ready prep
  • Slab surfacing for countertops and dining tables

Who books this service: Homeowners with a slab they want made into a dining table or countertop; furniture makers and woodworkers who don't own a sled; interior designers sourcing statement pieces.

Typical turnaround: 1–3 business days depending on queue.

03 · Precision

CNC & Laser Engraving

Laser engraving from $2 / minute run-time

Personalize wood goods with precision laser engraving, or have a custom CNC job cut and ready to finish. Pricing is two-fold: a per-unit rate on stock items, and machine run-time on custom work so you only pay for the time the machine actually runs on your piece.

Laser engraving starts at $2/min for project run-time to create the finished product. As a quick example, twelve passes on a large bowl runs roughly 16 minutes total. Most logos, names, and small designs come in well under that.

CNC machining is quoted per job. Send Drew the file or a sketch with rough dimensions and material, and he’ll respond with a fixed-price quote covering setup, run-time, and finishing.

  • Logos, monograms, and personalization on cutting boards and gifts
  • Custom signage and small-batch product runs
  • 2D CNC profiling, pocketing, and inlay prep
  • Vendor and maker work — bring your blanks, leave with finished parts

Who books this service: small businesses needing branded gifts; makers without a CNC or laser; homeowners wanting a one-off personalized piece.
